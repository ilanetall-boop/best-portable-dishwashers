Five AIRMSEN listings. Nearly one dishwasher.

Search "AIRMSEN portable dishwasher" on Amazon and you get five separate listings, four of which repeat the same bullet points, the same dimensions and the same tank size word for word, at four different prices. The fifth genuinely is a different machine. We pulled the published specs for all five ASINs to work out which is which.

AIRMSEN sells at least five separate dishwasher listings on Amazon right now, under five different ASINs: B08MWNQK1Z, B0CTQKMJ25, B0GWL6PH6C, B0GWL594RM and B0HCBZ52LC. Four of them — all except the last — are titled "AIRMSEN Portable Countertop Dishwasher, No Hookup Needed," use the same five product photos in different orders, and carry bullet copy that matches, word for word, down to the typo. The fifth is titled differently, "AIRMSEN Large Slim Countertop Dishwasher | Built-in Water Tank," and its spec sheet reads like a different appliance, because it is one.

None of this is unusual for small-appliance brands selling through Amazon, and none of it is necessarily deceptive on its own. What it does mean is that the normal shopping instincts — compare the listings, read the bullets, check what people say in the reviews — stop working, because four of the five listings are the same comparison repeated four times. This guide goes through the published specifications for all five ASINs, lines up what's genuinely identical against what's genuinely different, and works out which listing is worth buying at today's price.

The pattern

Why four of the five listings read almost word for word the same

Pull up the bullet points on B08MWNQK1Z, B0CTQKMJ25, B0GWL6PH6C and B0GWL594RM and you'll find the identical three bullets on all four, down to the same run-together word in the first line ("easilymeeting"). All four state the machine "measures just 16.75W x 16.86D x 18.05H inches," all four describe a "built-in 5-liter water tank" with an optional hose to the faucet, all four quote "5 washing programs including Normal, Speed, Glass, Baby Care and Fruit" plus "2 enhanced functions," and all four claim it washes "40+ items (up to 12-inch plates, but needs to be placed at a slight angle)."

That's not a coincidence of similar products from the same brand; it's the same listing copy, reused verbatim across four separate ASINs. Sellers do this for ordinary reasons: a new ASIN resets the listing's age and review history, a cosmetic variant sometimes has to be filed as its own ASIN rather than a variation on an existing one, and running the same unit under more than one listing lets a seller test price points without touching a page that's already ranking. In our database, two of the four ASINs (B0GWL6PH6C and B0GWL594RM) were already present in August 2026; the other two (B08MWNQK1Z and B0CTQKMJ25) were added in September — the lineup was extended over time, not launched all at once.

Whatever the reason on AIRMSEN's side, the effect for a shopper is the same: four listings that look like four choices are, on the spec sheet, one choice repeated. The real decision in this lineup isn't among those four — it's between that one machine and the genuinely different fifth listing, which we get to below.

What's actually different

Price, tub depth and tank size — and which of those three actually move

The brief for this kind of listing cluster is usually "check the price, the tub depth and the tank size." Here, two of those three don't move at all across four of the five ASINs, which is itself the finding. Per the published specs:

  • Tub depth: 16.86 inches on B08MWNQK1Z, B0CTQKMJ25, B0GWL6PH6C and B0GWL594RM — identical, to the hundredth of an inch, on all four. The fifth listing, the Large Slim, states a shallower 14.2-inch depth.
  • Tank size: a 5-liter built-in tank on all four standard listings, again identical. The Large Slim's bullets don't restate a liter figure, but describe the interior as 25 percent larger by volume with a taller, wider cavity — a bigger-capacity design built around the same no-hookup tank concept, not a bigger tank spec we can quote in liters.
  • Price: this is the one spec that actually separates the four standard listings, and it moves by nearly 70 percent — $201.36 on B08MWNQK1Z up to $339.99 on B0GWL594RM, for a product whose own listing copy says it's identical.

Color is the other real difference among the four: B08MWNQK1Z ships in white, B0CTQKMJ25 in green, B0GWL6PH6C in white & black, and B0GWL594RM in white. If you've been told to "compare the specs before you buy," the specs across these four already agree with each other — the only things left to compare are the price you'd pay and the color sitting in the product photo.

The real variant

The Large Slim, and what the extra $60 to $200 actually buys

The Large Slim (B0HCBZ52LC) is the one listing in the group that isn't a repeat. Its name is a fair description of the trade it makes: at 14.2 inches deep it's noticeably slimmer front-to-back than the 16.86-inch standard unit, which the listing pitches for narrow counters and shelves, but the interior is built wider and taller, adding a stated 25 percent more capacity and enough height to fit items up to 18.5 inches long — pans with handles, the listing specifically says, which the standard unit's flatter 12-inch-plate-at-an-angle capacity can't take.

The rack is genuinely different too: a 2-tier design holding up to 5 place settings and plates to 11.8 inches (with the upper rack removed), against the standard listing's "40+ items" figure that never resolves into a place-setting count. The program list is longer — 7 wash programs plus 2 extra options (Normal, Delicate, Heavy, Express at 28 minutes, Baby Care, Fruit at 9 minutes, and Self-Clean, plus Air Refresh and a 2-to-12-hour delay start) against the standard unit's 5 programs. The Large Slim's listing also specifies 3 high-pressure spray arms for 360-degree coverage and a 167°F high-temperature wash on most cycles, where the standard listing names no spray-arm count and no wash temperature at all.

One line in the Large Slim's own bullets is worth repeating rather than smoothing over: it asks for at least 23.5 inches of cabinet height for refilling the tank, versus the standard unit's 18.05-inch total height. If you were counting on this slotting into the same gap as the cheaper AIRMSEN, measure first — the size difference that buys the extra capacity also changes what will physically fit.

Whether that's worth $60 to $200 more than the standard listing depends on what's filling the tub. For two people with a normal day's dishes, the standard machine's 5-liter tank and 40-item capacity are plenty, and the Large Slim's extra reach is wasted. For a family that regularly runs a cycle with saucepans or a baking sheet in it, the taller rack and 18.5-inch item length are the one thing in this entire lineup that the cheaper listings genuinely cannot do, at any price.

The hidden cost

Why near-duplicate listings make price-tracking and reviews unreliable

Price-tracking tools and browser extensions that chart "price history" work ASIN by ASIN. They have no way to know that B08MWNQK1Z and B0GWL594RM are the same physical dishwasher — to the tool, they're two unrelated products that happen to share a brand name. A shopper who bookmarks one AIRMSEN listing and watches it for a drop can watch the wrong page for weeks while a cheaper, functionally identical ASIN sits a search result away the whole time.

The same split hits review aggregation, though in this lineup there's nothing yet to split: Amazon shows no rating and no review count for any of the five ASINs in our database. If that changes as the listings age, remember that four separate review counts for one physical product tell you less than one honest count would, because feedback about a unit bought under one ASIN never reaches a buyer comparing a different one. A listing that looks "unreviewed" might simply be a fresh ASIN for a product with a longer track record under a different one. None of this is evidence of anything sinister — splitting one product across ASINs happens for ordinary operational reasons — but it does mean the usual shortcuts, "check the reviews" and "watch the price history," both assume one listing per product, and this lineup doesn't give you that.

FAQ

Common questions about the AIRMSEN lineup

Are the five AIRMSEN listings actually the same dishwasher?

Four of them are, going by the published specs: B08MWNQK1Z, B0CTQKMJ25, B0GWL6PH6C and B0GWL594RM all list the same 16.75 × 16.86 × 18.05-inch dimensions, the same 5-liter built-in tank, the same 5-program wash list and the same 40-item capacity, word for word. The fifth, the AIRMSEN Large Slim (B0HCBZ52LC), is a different machine — shallower, with a taller and wider interior, a 2-tier rack, 7 wash programs and a higher price.

Why do four identical listings show different prices?

Each ASIN is priced independently on Amazon, so a seller running the same physical product under multiple listings can set, promote or discount each one separately, and prices on each can drift apart over time for reasons that have nothing to do with the product — stock levels, advertising spend, or simple inattention on an older listing. Nothing about the spec sheet changes when the price does.

What does the AIRMSEN Large Slim add over the standard model?

A shallower 14.2-inch depth paired with a taller, wider interior the listing describes as 25 percent larger by capacity, room for items up to 18.5 inches long such as pans with handles, a 2-tier rack holding up to 5 place settings with plates to 11.8 inches, 7 wash programs plus Air Refresh and a delay-start option, 3 spray arms, and a 167°F wash on most cycles. It also needs about 23.5 inches of cabinet clearance rather than the standard unit's 18.05 inches.

How do I know which AIRMSEN listing to buy right now?

If you want the standard machine, compare the live price across B08MWNQK1Z, B0CTQKMJ25, B0GWL6PH6C and B0GWL594RM and pick whichever is cheapest in the color you want — their specs are identical, so price and color are the only variables. Buy the Large Slim instead only if your dishes regularly exceed the standard unit's 40-item, 12-inch-plate capacity.
